# Urbanic | Project Scope & Ownership

## Project position
Brand and digital experience transformation of an established fashion product, spanning App, H5, Web, marketing and content touchpoints.

## Individual role
As core project lead for brand and product experience transformation, I managed the experience design team, translated company strategy into project objectives and scope, organized assignments, reviews and cross-functional collaboration, and drove delivery. I was also a principal contributor to the core UI/UX design, personally defining and producing key experiences, screens and cross-platform rules.

Official company role: Experience Design Expert.

## Boundaries of change
- Remove: selected promotional mechanisms that conflicted with the brand position.
- Simplify: retain useful functions with adjusted priority, visual weight, entry, frequency and interaction.
- Redesign: brand recognition, discovery, browsing and purchase decision experiences.
- Preserve: proven core bag, checkout, payment and order interactions, rules and exception handling, while refreshing visual presentation.

## Ownership boundaries
Company leadership set the overall strategy. As project lead, I coordinated objectives, scope, cross-functional collaboration and delivery for the brand and product experience transformation. Product management, fashion design, brand agencies, photography and engineering retained their respective specialist responsibilities.

## Metric definitions
- Over 50 million global users in 2024: internal project records; user definition not specified.
- Approximately 80% female users aged 18–35: internal audience analysis; research period and sample not specified.
- Approximately 30% increase in user dwell time: internal records; relative increase, comparison period and calculation not specified.
- Global GMV exceeded RMB 2 billion in 2023: overall business scale context.
- Gross margin increased by approximately 20–30% relatively in 2023: not 20–30 percentage points; baseline not specified.
- Commercial performance reflected product, pricing, supply chain, marketing, operations and experience, and is not solely attributable to design.

## Subsequent business observations
The project lead observed slowing growth and pressure on customer sentiment. Higher prices raised expectations for quality and service, while product and production investment increased costs and lengthened cycles. Without sustained, stable sales growth, operating pressure increased and the business gradually declined. This is a retrospective observation, not a substitute for financial or independent causal analysis.
