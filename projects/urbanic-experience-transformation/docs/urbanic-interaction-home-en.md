# Home & Navigation | Interaction Design

Prepared from project design screens and retrospective context. This document focuses on experience goals and decisions; specific behavior is subject to the original prototypes.

## Experience goal
Home supports brand recognition, content browsing and product discovery. The upgrade aimed to make Urbanic’s fashion and brand visible before customers continue into shopping, while keeping promotional information supportive rather than dominant.

## Typical journey
Enter home → explore brand content and product entry points → use navigation or page content to browse products → view an item of interest.

## Key design decisions
- Brand and product first: use hero imagery, typography, content layouts and product presentation to communicate the new brand.
- Clear entry points: organize navigation, content and product links into a recognizable hierarchy.
- Balanced promotion: adjust the number, priority and visual weight of marketing entries according to commercial value and brand position.
- Cross-platform coherence: App, mobile web and desktop share brand expression and content priorities while adapting layout and entry points to each device.

## Across devices
The desktop design uses horizontal space for brand imagery and content; the mobile home adapts its first screen and navigation to a smaller display. The brand remains coherent while layouts change by device.

## States and feedback
The available designs show home and navigation entry points. Menu expansion and closing, back and scroll behavior, loading and error feedback require confirmation against the original prototypes or product; this document does not assume the historical implementation.

## Tradeoff
The existing home needed to support brand, products, campaigns and promotions. The project reduced the visual dominance of selected promotional tools so customers could better understand the brand and product while retaining commercially useful entry points.
