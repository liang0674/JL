# Product Detail & Add to Bag | Interaction Design

Prepared from project design screens and retrospective context. Visible interface evidence is distinguished from experience decisions; specific behavior is subject to the original prototypes.

## Experience goal
Product detail helps customers move from discovery to purchase decisions. The upgrade aimed to communicate product value through clear imagery and information, while making price, variants and add-to-bag easy to understand.

## Typical journey
Open product detail → review images and information → check price and options → use the add-to-bag entry → continue into shopping.

## Key design decisions
- Product value first: use photography and layout to focus attention on the item and information relevant to purchase.
- Clear hierarchy: organize product name, imagery, price, options, product information and add-to-bag; promotion supports the decision.
- Recognizable action: desktop and mobile show product information and an add-to-bag entry, with layouts adapted to available screen space.
- Stable transaction capabilities: bag, checkout, payment and orders can receive a visual refresh while core interactions, business rules and exception handling remain stable.

## Visible in the designs
The available desktop screen shows product imagery, name, price, size selection and an add-to-bag button; the mobile screen shows product detail and a bottom action area. Available options, inventory rules and state feedback cannot be established from screenshots alone.

## States and feedback
Color and size selection, out-of-stock, missing options, add-to-bag success or failure, and the behavior of an action during scrolling require confirmation against the original prototype or product. These states are not asserted as implemented features here.

## Tradeoff
Product, promotion and conversion information previously competed for attention. The upgrade reorganized their hierarchy around product presentation and customer decisions; once customers entered the transaction flow, proven core logic was retained to reduce unnecessary experience and business risk.
