# Savana | Concise Market Requirements Document

V0.1 · October 27, 2026 · Reconstructed from early positioning material and the project owner’s experience.

This document summarizes the original business context and product direction. It is not an original historical document or new market research. Target audience percentages are planning inputs; later business performance is not used to retroactively justify the initial opportunity.

## 1. Opportunity

As Urbanic moved upmarket, different budgets, ages and price preferences needed distinct propositions. Savana offered accessible fashion for younger women, balancing quality expectations with self-belief, quality and cost-effectiveness.

The opportunity was to connect an existing audience with a distinct value proposition and digital experience. No unsupported market-size or growth forecast is assumed.

## 2. Audience and needs

The early target distribution was approximately 40% aged 16–24, 45% aged 25–32 and 15% aged 32–40. These are targets, not observed post-launch demographics; the supplied bands overlap at age 32 and require boundary confirmation.

Needs inferred from positioning: discover suitable styles within budget; evaluate quality through images, materials and options; browse and shop smoothly on mobile; understand prices, offers and participation terms. These are project-based interpretations, not findings from newly conducted interviews or surveys.

## 3. Cold-start challenges

- Brand understanding: give Urbanic customers a reason to consider another app through products, price, style and identity.
- Acquisition and migration: reduce friction in download, app handoff and account entry.
- Activation: connect completed login to exploration and purchasing motivation.
- Repeat visits: make discovery and price information understandable within a large catalog.
- Trust: keep promotional prices, eligibility, stock and final amounts explainable across screens.

## 4. Resources and constraints

The team could draw on Urbanic’s first-party audience, technology and commerce capabilities, proven shopping UX and supply-chain foundations. Audience size and overlap have not been quantified in the supplied material.

Platform Reuse + Experience Customization retained foundations and core UX, refined browsing, rebuilt Savana brand and UI, and customized growth products. Less than two months from design to launch describes shared delivery, not invented stage schedules or isolated efficiency gains.

## 5. Strategy and priorities

| Strategy | Need | Capability |
| Product value first | A reason to move | Savana product zone in Urbanic and cross-app acquisition |
| Low friction and immediate reward | Account entry and activation | Authorized login, account association / creation, Welcome Coupon |
| Ongoing value and discovery | Decisions and reasons to return | Homepage Flash Sale, product pool and time / stock / price information |
| Full-funnel campaign | Acquisition, scale and price understanding | Mega Promotion with consistent cross-screen rules |

This order describes roles and dependencies, not a historical engineering schedule or mandatory user sequence. Secure a usable shopping foundation, independent identity and account continuity, then connect promotional rules to commerce under the actual configuration. Corresponding PRDs define details.

## 6. Success criteria and validation

| Stage | Evaluation | Definitions needed |
| Acquisition | Clicks, marketplace arrival, install, first open | Deduplication, cross-app linkage, window |
| Migration | Authorization and login completion | Valid entrants and failure causes |
| Activation | Coupon, continued browsing, first purchase | Eligibility, activation event, purchase window |
| Conversion | Cart, checkout, submission, payment | Denominators, valid orders, cancellations and refunds |
| Retention | Revisits and repeat purchases | Cohorts, observation period and repeat-purchase definition |

No numerical targets or feature-level improvements are fabricated. Early outcomes and later public brand performance are presented separately with sources and attribution boundaries.

Validate market and currency, account and consent, offer eligibility and stacking, stock, payment and actual feature outcomes. The MRD establishes why and what; PRDs establish the executable rules.
