# Savana | 0→1 Growth Product System Overview PRD

V0.4 · October 26, 2026 · Retrospective reconstruction and proposed rules.

Confirmed feature descriptions, design proposals and open decisions are distinguished. This is not an original historical PRD or evidence that all proposed behavior shipped. Parameters require business confirmation.

## 1. Context and confirmed capabilities

Savana’s accessible fashion proposition complemented Urbanic’s move upmarket. The lifecycle framework connects Acquisition → Migration → Activation → Conversion → Retention, with major campaigns serving scale. My role was Product Design Lead — 0→1 & Growth, spanning product definition, growth experience, interaction, UI and reusable rules. Four confirmed capabilities connect existing traffic, account entry and promotions: the Urbanic Savana product module with app-marketplace / app navigation; authorized login and coupons for Urbanic customers; the core homepage Flash Sale module; and a storewide “1-yuan” campaign journey across the app.

The goal is a clear destination, understandable account entry and offer conditions, and consistent product, price and status feedback.

## Product architecture and delivery strategy (confirmed update)

The app moved from design to production launch in less than two months using Platform Reuse + Experience Customization. This describes shared team delivery, not a from-scratch engineering rebuild or an individual-only effort.

| Layer | Strategy | Scope |
| Technology Foundation | Extensive Urbanic reuse | Accounts, products, cart, checkout, payment, orders, stock, search, recommendation, backend and app framework |
| Core UX | Retain and refine | Browsing, search, filters, PDP, cart, checkout and orders |
| UI & Brand | Rebuild | Visual language, typography, color, layout, component appearance, imagery and feedback |
| Custom Product | Growth-specific customization | Cross-brand acquisition, migration and Welcome Coupon, Flash Sale and Mega Promotion |

Platform strategy was shared across the team. My role focused on experience prioritization, independent brand and UI, growth feature definition and interaction, and system-supported page delivery alongside product and engineering. Stage durations, cost savings and isolated efficiency percentages have not been supplied.

## 2. Module boundaries

| PRD | Start → end | Responsibility |
| Cross-brand acquisition | Urbanic product entry → valid Savana destination | Brand, destination and source context |
| Authorization and coupons | Consent explanation → account and entitlement outcomes | Identity, eligibility and issuance |
| Flash Sale | Homepage offer → promotional order outcome | Special-price discovery and purchase continuity |
| Storewide campaign | Campaign touchpoint → campaign order outcome | Cross-app-screen consistency and eligibility |

These are not mandatory sequential steps. Users may open Savana directly. Flash Sale and the storewide campaign are distinct offer contexts.

Newly confirmed: installed users use Deep Link and others App Store / Google Play; one-tap authorization associates or creates a Savana account with Welcome Coupon after login; Flash Sale uses a limited pool, countdown, stock and price difference; the major campaign spans the shopping chain. Technical implementation and parameters remain separate decisions.

## 3. Shared information and rules

Agree source and destination context, market and language, authorization transaction, coupon rules and final issuance result, SKU availability and price validity, and order totals and payment outcomes. Source tags are not credentials. Final identity, entitlement, inventory and amount decisions come from the responsible services.

Proposed: preserve recoverable context; distinguish canceled consent, failed login and failed issuance; explain changes before submitting an altered total. Post-install restoration, inventory reservation and offer stacking are not assumed.

## 4. Offer relationship decisions

| Combination | Decision required | Proposed experience |
| Authorization coupon × Flash Sale | Eligibility, threshold and price basis | Show applicability and reasons |
| Authorization coupon × campaign | Stacking, exclusions and threshold | Follow a single confirmed rule |
| Flash Sale × campaign | Concurrent participation and price priority | Display the actual effective price |
| Promotion × shipping | Shipping and other charges | Separate item discounts from fees |
| Markets and languages | Currency, timezone and scope | Use actual market configuration |

## 5. Collaboration and acceptance

Proposed ownership: design covers entry, hierarchy, states and recovery; product confirms business rules; client and service teams agree routing, authorization, coupons, pricing, inventory and order contracts; operations confirms configuration and messaging; analytics defines deduplication and attribution. Check against actual historical teams.

Validate destinations, recovery, canceled authorization, issuance queries, price continuity, changing conditions, ended offers, sold-out items and bilingual content. Measure each funnel separately with defined units, windows and attributable traffic. Overall sales and DAU are not causal evidence for a single feature.

## 6. Open decisions

Market, currency, platforms, links, account relationship, coupon terms, campaign schedule, product scope, limits, inventory reservation, price priority, shipping, payment, refunds and attribution windows. Confirm business rules, then interaction states, contracts and acceptance criteria.


## Updated framing and measurement boundary

Growth design connects Business Goal, User Motivation, Product Mechanism and Experience Design through clearer value, less effort, accessible prices and coherent shopping. Retention is the intended role of ongoing updates, not a verified uplift.
