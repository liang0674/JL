# Savana | Mega Promotion Low-price Growth Campaign PRD

V0.3 · October 26, 2026 · Retrospective reconstruction and proposed rules.

Confirmed feature descriptions, design proposals and open decisions are distinguished. This is not an original historical PRD or evidence that all proposed behavior shipped. Parameters require business confirmation.

## 1. Confirmed scope and boundaries

The major low-price growth campaign used “1-yuan purchase” as its internal concept, with goals of acquisition, activation, brand awareness and transaction scale; settlement currency, eligibility, product scope, limits, shipping and stacking are unresolved. The aim is consistent offer meaning, qualification and final-amount feedback across shopping.

## 2. Task and coverage map

Campaign touchpoint → understand rules → qualify → select product and SKU → cart → checkout validation → order / payment outcome. Entry may occur through actual homepage, list or product touchpoints, not necessarily one mandatory campaign page. The updated account confirms Home, PLP, PDP, Cart, Checkout, Payment and Order Success as the main chain, with exposure, discovery, decision and result layers. Specific layouts still need design evidence.

| Scenario | Information | Validation |
| Homepage / campaign entry | Benefit, essential terms, rules access | Active campaign and valid destination |
| Search / category / list | Product participation and offer | Labels match real scope |
| Product detail | Product eligibility, price and conditions | Customer and SKU applicability |
| SKU selection | Options, quantity and relevant price | Availability and limits |
| Cart | Participation, quantity and discount | Eligibility changes and conflicts |
| Checkout | Item amounts, discounts, fees and total | Final validation and reconfirmation |
| Order result / detail | Actual amount, order and next step | Payment and eligibility consumption |
| Coupons / related promotions | Applicability and reasons | Stacking and priority |

Shared mechanisms include Promotion Tag, Price Hierarchy, Qualification, Inventory, Limit, Countdown, Conflict and Failure. Earlier prices and offers should remain understandable at checkout.

## 3. Eligibility and usage

Explain necessary conditions. Qualification failures should provide actionable reasons. Login requirements, new-customer status, prior entitlement claiming and per-person, order or SKU limits are not assumed.

If eligibility can be consumed, define reservation, confirmation and release on cancellation, expiry or refund. Service state is authoritative; repeat submissions should not repeatedly consume the same entitlement.

## 4. Selection, cart and checkout

Show valid offer prices and available options within confirmed conditions. Revalidate stock, eligibility and price using agreed cart and checkout protocols. Explain affected items and offer edits. Confirm mixed carts, multiple campaign items and per-item applicability; do not silently replace products or offers.

Separate item subtotal, campaign discounts, other discounts, shipping / required fees and final amount. The campaign label must not obscure charges. Agree coupon and Flash Sale relationships and explain inapplicability. If conditions or totals change before submission, obtain review before continuing.

## 5. State and recovery matrix

| State | Proposed recovery |
| Missing login or eligibility | Explain conditions; use supported login, rules or normal shopping paths |
| Not active or ended | State the effective condition; exit or reconfirm regular purchase |
| SKU sold out | Disable it; allow another SKU or product |
| Limit reached or entitlement used | Explain; change quantity or query the associated order |
| Cart / checkout discrepancy | Identify products and amounts; request review |
| Repeat submission or timeout | Prevent duplicate action and query the order |
| Payment failure / unknown | Distinguish confirmed failure from pending lookup |
| Cancellation / refund | Explain actual eligibility and stock handling under agreed rules |

## 6. Measurement and acceptance

Track entry, rules, eligibility, selection, valid cart additions, checkout, submission, final payment, exits and recovery. Define eligible-user participation, cart and submission completion, payment outcomes and qualification / pricing interruption rates with explicit denominators and windows. Campaign orders are not automatically net-new users or long-term retention.

Validate all coverage scenarios for the same user and product, changing eligibility, SKU and quantity, activity expiry, stock, mixed carts, conflicts, fees and duplicate submissions. Confirm markets, currency, schedule, qualification, scope, limits, shipping, price priority, stock and eligibility reservation / release, orders, payments, refunds and real screen coverage.


## Updated framing and measurement boundary

Evaluation includes Campaign Exposure, Campaign CTR, New User Acquisition, Activation, Add to Cart, Order, Conversion, CAC and Repeat Purchase. CAC requires cost and valid acquisition definitions; repeat purchase requires an observation period. Feature-level outcomes have not been supplied.
