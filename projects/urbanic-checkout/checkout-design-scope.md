# 职责与设计交付说明

## 流程定义

持续负责三个版本的体验设计。梳理前置与后置场景，明确支付任务和信息展示顺序。

## 交互与组件设计

定义支付方式选择、输入字段、使用条件和反馈状态，将共用模块与各支付方式的差异整理成可复用的设计规则。

## 跨端 UI、UX 与视觉规范

完整负责 2.0 的 Web、H5 和 App，覆盖布局、信息层级、交互适配及与品牌升级方向一致的支付视觉语言。

## 研发协作

与研发对齐字段、状态、展示条件及跨端实现规则，推动设计交付。

# Role & Design Delivery Notes

## Flow definition

Own experience design across all three versions. Map both entry points and define payment tasks and information order.

## Interaction and component design

Define selection, fields, usage conditions and feedback. Turn shared modules and method-specific differences into reusable design rules.

## Cross-platform UI, UX and visual design

Own desktop web, mobile web and app for 2.0, including layout, hierarchy, responsive behavior and alignment with the brand refresh.

## Engineering collaboration

Align fields, states, display conditions and cross-platform implementation rules with engineering.
